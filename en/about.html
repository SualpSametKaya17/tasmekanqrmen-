About Us

Taşmekan Cafe & Patisserie opened its doors in 2020 as the meeting point of flavor and pleasure in Alsancak, a region of Northern Cyprus that stands out with its natural beauties. Since the day we were founded, our aim has been to create not only a cafe, but also a warm environment where friendships flourish, memories accumulate and our guests of all age groups can feel at home.

Our extensive menu combines both traditional flavors that appeal to local palates and original tastes inspired by world cuisine. We offer a choice for every taste with our daily freshly prepared cakes, handmade desserts, carefully served coffee varieties and seasonal beverage alternatives. In addition, we accompany your pleasant conversations with our hookah service, which is one of the indispensables of our guests.

Taşmekan makes a difference not only with its taste but also with its peaceful atmosphere. We aim to offer you a space where you can breathe away from the stress of city life with our large seating areas, our stone architecture that preserves its natural texture and our simple, elegant decoration we offer in the interior. Your comfort is our priority in our cool garden in summer and in our warm interior on winter days.

Thanks to our experienced and hospitable team, our friendly service understanding and our constantly renewing structure, as Taşmekan Cafe & Patisserie, we aim to welcome you not only once but every time.

If you want to take a short break from the hustle and bustle of life, spend quality time with your loved ones or just give yourself a little reward, we welcome you to Taşmekan. We are happy to be here with every cup of coffee, every sweet slice, every smile.
